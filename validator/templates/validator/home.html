Why this exists

The same shipment can appear slightly differently across multiple documents.

Export shipments contain overlapping information across multiple documents. When important fields differ between documents, someone has to identify the discrepancy and decide whether it needs correction.

Export Shipment Control is being developed around that specific workflow: compare important fields, surface exceptions and make follow-up easier.

The workflow

From a stack of documents to a focused exception list.

  1. 01

    Compare

    Line up important shipment fields across the key documents being reviewed.

  2. 02

    Find exceptions

    Identify information that does not match and needs human review.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Record the discrepancy and follow it through to resolution.
